The Journey of AIR

From a simple idea to a globally recognized impact – explore the milestones that define our organization's roadmap.

June 2023

The Seed

It all starts here

Three ambitious freshmen boys try to start an AI club at their high school.

October 2023

Beginnings of AIR

Pivot in plans

Other students beat them to the punch and got an AI club running before they could. Undeterred, they decide to pivot, moving above students to work with teachers.

November 2023

First Teacher Coached

First test run

A local 4th grade teacher decided to work with them. She was the first ever teacher AIR collaborated with, and her class was AIR's first real AI in education experience.

April 2024

Five Oaks AI Summit

AI Seminar

After expanding their efforts to work with more teachers, AIR was invited to be the only student presenters at the Five Oaks AI Summit. There they met new people and reshaped their identity into a larger more unified organization.

June 2024

Collaboration with AI Advantage Agency

AI Course

After meeting AJ Green, CEO of AI Advantage Agency, a deal was made. AIR would create courses and certify teachers with the funding and support of the company. 68 teachers received full certifications after completing our 8 week AI in Education course.

August 2024

New Members and New Visions

AI Development - Project K

Through the continuous efforts of the amazing AIR team, the organization has grown to 8 members, as AIR starts doing more software development through Project K.

September 2024

New Schools and More Teachers

Course Pilot and Larger Efforts

With more experience under their belt, the team grew more, expanding to 12 members. Now working with entire schools during professional development days, word was spreading about our impact.

January 2025

Writing District Policy

AI Policy

AIR's efforts were quickly recognized by the school district, as AIR was invited to collaborate on the GenAI committee. Additionally through one-on-one meetings, AIR was able to help add student centered ideologies to the AI policy.

July 2025

Starting New Chapters

Expansion

With so many resources gathered, AIR thought it was time to take their experiences and offer them to other students. Taking every slide, email, course, project, and software they created, AIR made a guide on how to start your very own AIR chapter in your own city.

xx 2026

What Happens Next?

Future

AIR has now grown larger than anyone could have ever expected, but they're only just getting started. AIR has ambitious goals to add more chapters and create governmental level impact. Although AIR has never applied for 501(c)(3) because the organization doesn't handle money, one day AIR plans to be a globally renowned non-profit organization.
