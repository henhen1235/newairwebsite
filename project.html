Our Projects

Learn about some of the projects we have been working on!

NWRED CAfE Summit

Presenting at the Northwest Regional Education District (NWRED) CAfE Summit, AIRethought shared our insights on integrating AI in classrooms with a focus on equity. AIR provided teachers with practical usecases for students, AI tools, and curriculum development to foster an inclusive learning environment.

AIR Project-K

Project K is an affordable AI-integrated learning platform designed for schools. It provides transparency, built-in mental-health support, and comprehensive teacher training, all ready for immediate deployment. With proven success partnering with major U.S. school districts, Project-K aims to democratize high-quality education, enhance learning outcomes, and prepare students and educators for an AI-driven future.

School Wide Survey

AIR conducted a school wide survey to assess the impact of AI tools on student learning and engagement. The survey collected feedback from students in high school to identify strengths and areas for improvement in AI integration within the curriculum. The results will inform future program development and support strategies. If you want to see our data, please contact.

AIR Certification Course

Working with AI Advantage Agency, AIR created an AI certification course for educators. This course equips teachers with the knowledge and skills to effectively integrate AI tools into their teaching practices, enhancing student learning experiences and outcomes. There is no fully comprehensive course with insights like ours available.
