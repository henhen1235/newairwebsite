OSU's Oregon Broadband Equity Coalition

Bridging the digital divide through AI education

Together, we're working to ensure every student and educator across Oregon has access to the AI literacy resources they need to thrive in a digital future.

Bringing AI to Every District in Oregon

A joint initiative to close the broadband and AI literacy gap statewide

Why This Partnership Matters

Oregon's rural and underserved communities face a compounding challenge — limited broadband access and a lack of AI literacy resources. Through this collaboration, AIRethought and OSU's Oregon Broadband Equity Coalition are combining expertise to directly address both.

Our joint program deploys student-led AI literacy workshops into districts that have historically been left behind, giving teachers the tools and confidence to guide their students through the rapidly evolving AI landscape.

From the coast to eastern Oregon, no district will be left behind.
