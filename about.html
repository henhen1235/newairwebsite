About AIRethought

The future of education is AI.

AIRethought is a student-led organization based out of Portland, Oregon. First started in June 2023, AIRethought has dedicated their time and effort to advancing AI literacy in classrooms. The AIRethought team believes that the future of education is one where teachers are assisted by AI tools.
That's what AIRethought does: teach teachers about AI.
