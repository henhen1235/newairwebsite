AIR PROJECT-K: The AI tool that support teacher/student transparency

What It Is

Project-K is a custom AI ecosystem designed to enhance transparency between teachers and students in educational settings. It provides teachers with the ability to monitor student AI usage, ensuring responsible and ethical integration of AI tools in the classroom.

Development Effort

  • 30000+ lines of code supporting infrastucture
  • 1000+ hours coding the platform
  • Research backed with published results on effectiveness

Testing & Validation

  • Project-K piloted at two schools
  • Endorsed by Almbani School district
  • Currently used at CEC and SAS
