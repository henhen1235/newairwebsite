Preparing Educators to Navigate AI in Classrooms

AIRethought (AIR for short) is a student-led organization dedicated to providing AI literacy support to teachers.

Login About Us

AI is Transforming Education
But Teachers Need Support

Students are already using AI

ChatGPT, AI homework tools, and more. Teachers need to understand these tools to guide students effectively. Many students use AI without understanding its limitations or ethical implications, making teacher guidance essential.

Schools lack clear policies

Districts struggle with questions about academic integrity, data privacy, and appropriate use of AI tools. Without clear guidelines, schools face inconsistent enforcement and confusion among staff, students, and parents.

No standardized training exists

Teachers are left to navigate AI alone without professional development or support systems. The vast majority of K-12 teachers have received zero formal training on AI, leaving them unprepared to guide students.

Opportunity for innovation

When properly trained, teachers can leverage AI to enhance learning and personalize education. AI can help differentiate instruction and handle administrative tasks, freeing teachers to focus on what matters most.

Our Solution

01

AI Literacy Support

  • Collaborate with our network of student experts to understand AI's role in modern education
  • Access professional development programs and earn AI teaching certifications

AI Policy Development

  • Partner with our experienced team to craft custom AI policies tailored to your district's needs
  • Implement proven frameworks for responsible AI integration in classrooms
  • Develop student-centered guidelines that promote ethical and effective AI use
03

AI Platform

  • Pilot our proprietary AI platform designed specifically for K-12 classrooms
  • Benefit from complete transparency between students and educators in all AI interactions
  • Experience technology built by students, refined by educators, and perfected for everyone
